**DECISION PREPARATION ONLY — NO CONSTRUCTION, DEPLOYMENT, ACTIVE TESTING, OR TIER B EXECUTION AUTHORIZED**

**EXTERNAL-INPUT HOLD — ARCHITECTURE C IMPLEMENTATION NOT AUTHORIZED**. These are proposed D3 interpretation constraints. No higher-class evidence has been newly admitted or qualified.

Progression is not an automatic ladder or simple relabeling of old data. Each source must satisfy the destination claim's acquisition, configuration, admissibility and approval conditions; retrospective equivalence may be inadequate if mandatory evidence was never collected. Matching one source/version/property is not whole-rig equivalence. Missing FR01–FR20/A3/applicable E9 originals cannot be reconstructed from EXT excerpts or an owner summary. RRM MAP identifiers aid tracing; controlled D2/SRS criteria retain authority. No numerical tolerance or approved test count is created here.

Unsupported claims explicitly prohibited:

- The modified rig is equivalent to controlled F00 without approved complete equivalence evidence.
- The original startup root cause has been conclusively identified: the observed ordering/deadline/latch mechanism does not resolve exclusive scene/resource/transport/DDS subcauses or requirement fit.
- A single startup establishes statistical repeatability, p95, reliability, causal superiority or a distribution.
- Proposed isolation has been verified by a drawing, monitoring status, absent FD, domain label, source template or owner assertion alone.
- Architecture approval constitutes Tier B execution approval or target designation.
- Engineering diagnostic results automatically close G0 or original frozen findings.
