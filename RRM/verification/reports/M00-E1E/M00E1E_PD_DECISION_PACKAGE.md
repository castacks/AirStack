## Required separation

D1–D5 grant no construction, deployment or active testing. Detailed design, construction/configuration, deployment, passive observation of an already-running environment, active startup verification and Tier B trials each require their own explicit scope; they cannot inherit authority from each other. E1E permits the bounded local records review already performed, not a continuing live-observation authority. Any later protected implementation requires reviewable concrete diffs and explicit authority. Any startup counts as startup, including readiness preparation, setup or characterization; no hidden warm-up/retry. No target designation, Tier C, WP01, G0 progression, RA01 approval or push is implied.

Smallest external action: PD completes D4 with an accountable infrastructure/safety point of contact and authorized routing for request Q1, and obtains the existing approved execution-model/security/delegation records. Remaining Q2–Q6 are role-routed evidence requests, not instructions to provision or test. With none supplied, stop E1E on external-input hold. This is a prepared decision package, not an assertion that owner-dependent decisions are ready for unconditional approval.
