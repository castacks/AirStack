Use the M00-D proposed event contract and offline `RRM/scripts/m00d_validate_events.py`: episode, trial/attempt, workflow, producer, process-instance, PID, boot, sequence, instrumentation ID, event name, clock domain, native integer timestamp, unit, source/provenance, receipt timestamp, parent event, missing/drop/error indicators. Units and clock-domain identities are mandatory. Retain raw bytes and extraction tool revision alongside normalized JSONL and event coverage. Format-valid traces are not acquisition completeness or qualification.

Clock domains: process steady nanoseconds for guard and local elapsed spans; kernel boot ticks with tick rate for process lifecycle; application producer realtime timestamps; Docker receipt realtime separately; simulator time with reset/epoch for simulation events; packet/capture clock identified explicitly. Capture monotonic–realtime brackets at recording start/end and resets; record mapping uncertainty and observed drift. Shared printed UTC does not establish synchronization. Keep simulation time out of wall-startup deltas. Cross-process/domain subtraction is allowed only with a reviewed mapping and uncertainty, otherwise mark UNMEASURABLE. Never replace missing native origin with first log receipt. Record restart/PID reuse and boot changes as new identities.

Proposed acquisition begins before the first allowed process start and continues through the reviewed observation/stop window, including failures. Capture lifecycle/guard/communication events on transition, not inferred from polling; resource samples proposed at 1 Hz and GPU at 1 Hz. These are acquisition proposals, not acceptance limits. Higher resolution, packet filters, capture byte limits, maximum runtime, storage reserve, async buffer sizes and overhead budget must be frozen and approved under CHK10–CHK14 before execution. No high-rate payload logging on control callbacks; use bounded asynchronous queues with explicit dropped/late/error counters. Record actual cadence and observer overhead. If sampling is unavailable or gaps occur retain the attempt, flag the missing channel and stop if required evidence is lost. No resource stress variation is authorized or included in the initial matrix.

The independent recorder must remain separately supervised from executor/trial parent and retain ordered records, process exit and recorder health before/after abort. E1 does not demonstrate REC-01 survivability. Do not perform a kill/fault test under E1. Live hooks and packet capture require source and capability review; receive-only capture never permits synthetic heartbeat/state publications.

Preserve exact historical observations: deadline error to MAVROS connection **19.276162179 seconds**; application completion to vehicle graph **74.228771713 seconds**. Both concern one historical startup. The first is not total PX4 startup; the second is a broad pre-PX4 interval, not exclusive scene-load time. The guard numeric steady origin, startup resource history and complete discovery/callback history were not recorded. These intervals are not acceptance thresholds or population estimates. The historical 10.0s budget, 0.5s freshness and 200ms timer describe implementation, not adopted requirements; E4 readiness-at-least-30s entry remains a separate source criterion.
