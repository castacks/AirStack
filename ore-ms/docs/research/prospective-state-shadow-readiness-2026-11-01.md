## Claim levels and what unknown training means

Unknown checkpoint membership does **not by itself block a narrower scoped
project benchmark**. The checker has a positive synthetic case demonstrating
that, when actual exposure/adapter/reference/input requirements are satisfied,
benchmark readiness can pass while network-held-out and physical claims stay
false. This is a software fixture, not evidence that the real candidates qualify.

A network-training-held-out claim additionally needs recording-disjoint evidence
for both exact checkpoints. Physical accuracy additionally needs an applicable,
independent physical pose/frame/time contract. A passed provided-reference
benchmark alone establishes neither. If prior exposure cannot be established,
any eventual reuse must be explicitly declared exploratory/uncertain-exposure
rather than silently branded prospective. None of these stronger claims is
supported by the present readiness report.
