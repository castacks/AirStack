## Finding executable evidence

Research scripts remain at their frozen paths under `ore-ms/tools/` and
`ore-ms/MAC-VO-main/Scripts/Setup/`. Locate a study through its findings or experiment
record, then follow its source pins, runtime contract and Box packet. Filenames
alone do not establish which version passed qualification. Independent auditors
are separate controls, not duplicate runners. Preserve original failed attempts.
