Verification consumes a capture from the simulator and uses its own projected rectangle pixel bounds, integer offset ray-length derivation, direct affine RGB transmission expression, and Decimal Simpson spectral integral. It includes zero-distance and zero-extinction identity, bounded transmission, the optically thick limit, a half-transmission slab, temperature and emissivity checks, zero response, foreground visibility and radiance, and seeded replay. Verify registration over the full frame by checking every shared pixel and selected modality radiances against separate references.

Numerical acceptance uses at most 2e-5 relative LWIR integration error for the tested spectra at 128 intervals, bounded by the smooth-grid refinement study, and a fourfold refinement error-ratio window of 3.8–4.2 for observed second-order convergence. The ratio is a diagnostic for this integration rule and the given scene, without a camera accuracy implication. Check the alternate Simpson integral at 1024 versus 2048 intervals within 1e-9 W·m⁻²·sr⁻¹. RGB identity and opaque-class checks are exact, with floating-point comparison at seven decimal places for distances and analytic RGB evaluations.
