# Research roadmap

M01 covers deterministic forward models, evaluation and planning. M02 would establish measured sensor and scene contracts and extend radiometry. M03 would implement classical stochastic estimation and forecasting after observability analysis. M04 would compare learning, physics-informed residuals and calibrated fusion on frozen splits. M05 would qualify physical transfer, compute, fault behavior and deployment evidence on independent conditions. Each future milestone requires separate authorization. Task-level ordering is in the work breakdown and dependency files.
