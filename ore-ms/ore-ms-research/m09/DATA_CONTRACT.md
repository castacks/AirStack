# M09 data and inference contract

M09-R1 conditionally accepts M08 and closes AC18 before implementation. AC10, AC17 and AC19 remain open. M07's estimator remains restricted to constrained effective burden, and M08's five-coefficient latent field remains distinct from the nonlinear path average. Historical data, baselines, protocols and result checksums are immutable.

Training uses 768 fresh synthetic realizations and validation uses 256 additional realizations under the unchanged M08 stationary process, reference path, and M07 detector likelihood. The small cohort is the first 128 training IDs. The final test is the exact original **128 M08 holdout realizations**, with all issue variants and horizons grouped by realization. Seeds and ID ranges are frozen and disjoint. All views of one realization remain in one partition.

The deployable scorer accepts exactly case ID, realization ID, split, and four issue detector codes. ID and split are join keys. They do not enter the network, normalization or novelty computations. Case-ID guards encode permitted context only. For matched, wrong-air, and wrong-dynamics controls, case ID has no effect on the model. Dropped, rail or invalid channels become masked. Known unusable association or all-missing channels trigger a tagged common prior fallback. Unknown range/material, externally declared unsupported domain and unqualified dynamics abstain. Unconstrained current air excludes LWIR. These are fixed eligibility rules, with no adaptive modality weighting or fusion policy.

Future burden and transmissions are separate supervision/evaluation labels. M08 issue-based teacher moments are a separate **training-only** input to the physics loss. Neither teacher data, future labels, oracle coefficients, simulator truth, future observations, nor the true forecast burden enter deployable inference. Isolated runtime verification omits all such files. M08 baseline and oracle forecasts are scorer-only, with oracle explicitly privileged.
