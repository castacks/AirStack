# M09 probabilistic neural formulation

Each network consumes four normalized issue detector codes and four availability indicators. Training-only centering and scale statistics are frozen per data size. The 24-unit tanh hidden layer maps those eight inputs to horizon-specific means and positive scales. Data has 966 parameters and predicts independent burden, RGB 550 nm transmission and LWIR 11 µm transmission logistics. Ablation and Physics each have 466 parameters and predict burden only. They apply the same accepted fixed-path RGB and LWIR Beer–Lambert maps to each burden sample. These preserve the joint optical relationship within a horizon, without supplying a learned multispectral fusion policy.

At each horizon z=logit(b) has a Gaussian forecast with σ=0.05+softplus(raw). The logistic transform returns b∈[0,1]. Sample using 128 fixed normal midpoint quantiles and map to each measurement space. Data applies the logistic to independently predicted z values for each target. Two-band forecasts in the coupled models satisfy T₅₅₀=exp[−10(0.003+0.135b)] and T₁₁=exp[−10(0.002+0.024b)]. The distinct coefficient scales are from the unchanged M05/M07/M08 public model. These are predicted direct transmissions along a nominal known 10 m ray. Current occlusion is an eligibility condition. Thermal crossover and clipping are observation limitations, with no geometric mutation.

All models minimize mean Gaussian NLL of supervised logits, omitting the constant term. Physics alone adds 0.5 times KL(N(μ_teacher,σ_teacher²) || N(μ_model,σ_model²)) over burden horizons. The teacher uses M08's unchanged issue-only conditioning and exact OU propagation of five correlated spatial coefficients, then matches mean and variance in logit space. This regularizer supplies a physical prediction reference without fitting a scalar Markov transition to burden. The ablation removes only this penalty, retaining its architecture, optics, batch order and supervision. The teacher's Gaussian projection has measured mean absolute burden-quantile differences of 0.00260–0.00838 from the original empirical forecasts on frozen M08 holdout projections. It is a training approximation and the original M08 samples remain the classical benchmark. [Teacher projection audit](evidence/teacher_projection.csv)

Future supervision is used only during training and validation. A deployed model estimates horizon-wise marginals rather than coherent trajectories. The dispersion combines effects of process variability and issue ambiguity under the training distribution. Initialization variation, teacher projection, sampled quantiles and model mismatch are reported separately. No complete epistemic or aleatoric decomposition is asserted. Novelty distance is a training-only, mask-specific Mahalanobis warning. It has no access to the future dynamics that change after issue time. Hard input gates and stationary prior fallback are identical across models.

CRPS scores the empirical continuous predictive distribution. Brier score and reliability bins separately assess the two M08 events. All paired intervals describe independent-realization score variation conditional on the three trained initializations. They do not establish physical performance or simultaneous coverage across all comparisons. [Proper scoring reference](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf)
