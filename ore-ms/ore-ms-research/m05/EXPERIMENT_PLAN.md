# M05 frozen experimental protocol

M05-R1 authorizes synthetic environmental state evolution only. Read the [roadmap mandate](../RESEARCH_ROADMAP_MANDATE.md) and the original specification §§3.1–3.2, 5–6. Starting revision is 21eaa450c. MATH-11–14 supply forcing regimes, random fields, SDE evolution and discretization, and MATH-09 supplies the range/extinction ambiguity check. PHY-25 sensor faults are outside this environmental experiment. Freeze the configuration, populations and criteria before implementation.

The domain is a 64 m periodic transect with 16 sampled positions at 4 m spacing. A finite Fourier field uses weights (0.2, 0.5, 0.3) for constant, fundamental, and second harmonic modes. Each latent coefficient follows an exact Gaussian OU step. Temperature uses a separate independent coefficient field. Use fixed seeds, independently initialized stationary realizations, and the named ensemble arms in `config.json`. Sample co-moving lags 0/2/6/12 s and separations 0/4/8/16/24 m. Compare coarse and fine exact transitions in law and with a coupled common innovation. Run three forced states (clear, driven, recovery), a prescribed moving deterministic profile, a stochastic synthetic fog analog, an effective dust analog with a separate optical parameterization, and a long-horizon stress matrix.

Use 4096 realizations per stationary arm, 512 for forced population statistics, and 16 long-horizon realizations for 512 steps each. Retain every stationary/forced statistical realization, selected field trajectories, and individual long-horizon extrema. Calculate mean, known-zero-mean variance and covariance, normalized correlations and pointwise normal-approximation 99% CIs from independent realization contributions (mean ±2.576 sample SE). CIs are descriptive and pointwise. Acceptance tolerances use fixed error budgets rather than simultaneous CI inclusion. At N=4096, the worst-case normalized covariance SE is √(2/N)≈0.022, and the mean SE is 0.016. Forced-process mean tolerance accounts for the smaller ensemble.
