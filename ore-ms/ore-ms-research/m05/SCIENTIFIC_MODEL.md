# M05 stochastic environmental state

## Dynamics and spatial covariance

The state consists of two finite sets of Gaussian coefficients, one for dimensionless optical burden and one for dimensionless air-temperature departure. Each coefficient obeys dA=−(A−μ)dt/τ+σ√(2/τ)dW. Within a regime the mean μ is constant, τ is a time in seconds, σ is the stationary coefficient standard deviation, and the Wiener innovations are independent across coefficients and fields. The exact conditional update has decay a=exp(−Δt/τ), conditional mean μ+a(A−μ), and innovation standard deviation σ√(1−a²). With stationary initialization the variance is σ². Deterministic evolution sets σ=0 and bypasses random draws. Prescribed regime changes at 12 and 36 s change μ while retaining a continuous state at each switch. The time grids align with the forcing and sample times. The implementation uses an exact conditional transition within a regime, with no Euler step bias. [Särkkä and Solin, linear SDE statistics and exact transition](https://users.aalto.fi/~asolin/sde-book/sde-book.pdf)

For period L=64 m, the world coordinate x is periodic. The co-moving coordinate is ξ=(x−ut) mod L, with prescribed transport speed u in m/s. Evaluate the constant coefficient with √w₀, and each cosine/sine pair of mode n with √wₙ and angular phase 2πnξ/L. The weights (0.2,0.5,0.3) add to one, so the latent covariance in the stationary case is

`Cov(F(x,t),F(x+h,t+lag)) = σ² exp(−|lag|/τ) K(h−u lag)`

where `K(h)=0.2+0.5 cos(2πh/L)+0.3 cos(4πh/L)`. The first e-fold spatial separation is 9.949 m. This is a characteristic scale of a periodic kernel, with negative lobes and exact recurrence at L. It is not a decay length for an unbounded atmosphere. Co-moving temporal correlation isolates τ, whereas fixed-location correlation also changes with transport. The regular output grid samples every 4 m. Off-grid values use the continuous Fourier expression instead of interpolation across discretized values. Periodic wrap is exact in the represented modes, with no open-boundary flux or turbulence closure. The forcing can add or remove burden and does not enforce conservation of material.

## Physical map and unit contract

The logistic transform maps burden b to [0,1] and maps the independent heat latent to 250–330 K by affine scaling after the logistic. Floating-point tails can approach either endpoint. This is a bounded nonlinear map, with no post-hoc clipping or rejection of Gaussian draws. Physical marginals are logistic-normal up to affine transformations and do not retain Gaussian moments or the exact latent covariance. The nominal temperature field uses τ=18 s, while stationary burden arms use 6 or 12 s. Samples and stress extrema retain physical bounds.

- RGB extinction at 550 nm is a declared floor plus span times burden, both in m⁻¹. Scattering is the declared single-scattering fraction times extinction, and absorption is the difference. The M02 adapter may use the provided power-law exponent to evaluate another wavelength. An incident scatter field remains a separate downstream input.
- LWIR absorption at 8, 11 and 14 µm uses different floors and spans in m⁻¹. The M03 kernel uses these with the separate kelvin state and path length in m. No visible coefficient is reused for thermal absorption.
- M04 still consumes geometric inputs. The environment records x and time with prescribed velocity, but emits no obstruction, depth or visibility mutation. A standalone unchanged ray check verifies this contract in isolation.

Fog and dust analogs deliberately share the same latent trace for a sensitivity comparison and have different scattering fractions and optical coefficient mappings. The common burden makes RGB and LWIR co-vary in each analog by construction. Temperature innovations are independent. These are effective synthetic analogs, with no aerosols, droplet-size distributions, settling, deposition, hydrometeor interactions, or chemistry modeled. Positive clear floors are retained. The fixed 5 m transfer probe shows that equal optical depth can result from different path-length/extinction combinations. Inferring range or environment from observations alone is outside this scope.

Process noise perturbs environmental dynamics and persists across space and time. Sensor noise is a distinct observation-stage term and is absent here. The five stationary ensembles are independently initialized and tested across realizations. No single trajectory is used to establish ergodicity. Stationarity applies only where means and transition parameters are fixed and stationary initial conditions are used. The externally forced examples are nonstationary. All reported parameters and bounds are synthetic. No calibrated forecast or real-weather validation is claimed.
