## Minimum viable bench

- **Optical geometry.** Use one rigid stationary RGB/LWIR mount, one surveyed line of sight through the controlled region, and one fixed target observable in both bands. Record camera-to-target distance, calibrated intrinsics and extrinsics, ROI coordinates, reference beam geometry and optical windows. Align the sampled target regions and the reference path. If the reference must use a nearby path, include spatial mismatch in the uncertainty budget. Fix target reflectance and illumination for RGB, and measure or bound emissivity, temperature, reflected background and path radiance for LWIR.
- **Independent label.** Use a calibrated visible transmission instrument with a stable source and independent detector, or a lab-qualified equivalent. Record detector, dark/background and source-monitor data, and normalize the dark-corrected, source-corrected path response to its clear-path response. Preserve raw values, band weighting, uncertainty and timestamps alongside the dimensionless estimate. Qualify any optical contribution from the reference source to the camera ROIs. The label path stays outside predictor inputs.
- **Regime and safety.** Select one regime the lab can bound and reset under a documented operating envelope. Specify the containment and environmental monitoring the lab requires, the allowed range of path conditions, controls for stable temperature and illumination, clear pre/post measurements, and stop authority if containment, instrument operation or environmental bounds fail. Log disturbances and controller states in the operator record. The lab must establish the applicability of the scattering and absorption approximations for the selected regime before holdout. [Acceptance conditions AC03–AC06, AC11 and AC15](ACCEPTANCE_CONDITIONS_REGISTER.md)
- **Timing and recorder.** Prefer a common trigger and event counter accepted by both cameras and the reference DAQ, with measured exposure and integration timing. A separately calibrated clock mapping is eligible if it meets the same timing budget. Use one acquisition host with verified native camera and DAQ interfaces. Preserve native buffers and device timestamps. Also log host receipt timestamps, sequence numbers, clock mappings, frame/trigger matches, dropped or repeated frames and device reset events. Host receive time alone does not establish exposure time.
- **Capture and scoring records.** Preserve raw RGB and radiometric LWIR, fixed gain/exposure/NUC settings, device and lens identifiers, calibration and clock receipts, geometry and ROIs, environmental records, instrument traces, clear controls, and a complete episode ledger. Prepare separate issue, label, controller and audit files. Predictor-visible issue records contain only eligible observations and frozen public metadata. The operator or scorer holds future labels and disturbance schedules until forecast files are locked. [M12 evidence handoff](M12_STATIONARY_FORECAST_PROTOCOL.md)

## Ordered acquisition and qualification plan

1. **Confirm resources on paper.** The Project Director identifies a lab owner. Record each device's model, serial, lens, raw format, manual operating modes, spectral response, calibration status/expiry, trigger and data interface, custody and booking window. Record the independent reference and facility approval owner in the same receipt. Resolve any unavailable dependency before selecting a purchase or booking.
2. **Review the physical design.** Produce the path/ROI drawing, optical and clock interfaces, instrument list, controlled variables and lab review packet. Assemble only under the approved qualification scope. Keep motion and vehicle integration outside this plan.
3. **Qualify measurement and calibration.** Under separate authorization, bind detector output to response, check rails and drift, survey/align the optics and demonstrate independently referenced transmission with the planned uncertainty. Document the approximation envelope and clean controls.
4. **Qualify synchronization.** Measure trigger-to-exposure and reference integration timing, verify offset/drift and drops, and establish the clock and trigger association across the complete intended window. Record all failures.
5. **Verify adapter and scorer.** Under approved scope, use nonphysical software fixtures first. Compare against frozen M08 numerical outputs where inputs are equivalent, then check separately versioned physical binding with available calibration evidence. Verify causal delivery, units, masking, persistence, fallback and label isolation.
6. **Acquire only an authorized clear-path dry run.** Use a stationary repeatable target and accepted timing/reference path. Verify native buffers, ID completeness, timestamps, rail flags, host throughput, replay and separation of issue and labels. Treat this as a recorder and metrology check. It does not justify forecast efficacy or enable attenuation exposure.
7. **Complete independent readiness review.** Present certificate, geometry, timing and adapter receipts with immutable digests, clear-dry-run evidence, the approved operating envelope and unresolved exceptions. Obtain an explicit disposition before any attenuation campaign.
8. **Schedule M12 execution under separate approval.** Follow only the agreed development and holdout sequence, including the cap of 12 development and 24 held-out attenuation episodes over three preassigned holdout sessions, clear controls, locked forecasts and independent scoring. A stage failure returns to review with the retained evidence.

**Resource planning.** The minimum apparatus is one RGB camera, one radiometric LWIR camera, one independent visible reference, a timing/trigger or calibrated common-clock system, one fixed geometry and target, one approved attenuation facility, and one acquisition machine with sufficient interfaces and storage. M08's reference predictor runs as a standard-library Python batch program. Bench processing rate and timing still require measurement. Size raw storage from the measured sum of each stream's bytes per sample × sample rate × full scheduled duration, including clear controls, file overhead, and retained replicas. Prices, procurement and calibration lead times, lab time, actual frame formats, data volume and device availability are unknown. No procurement estimate is warranted until the inventory receipt is available. [M08 reproduction notes](m08/REPRODUCTION.md)
