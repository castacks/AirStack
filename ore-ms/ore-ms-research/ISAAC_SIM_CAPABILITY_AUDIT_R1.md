# ORE-MS Isaac Sim capability audit R1

26 November 2026 UTC. AirStack `02b290ea008d34f4320d45f4dffb15e96a03fa7e`, Pegasus gitlink `8c7a664095396bc9c4f4609531445d4cf6390fe4`, Isaac Sim 5.1.0. Retained R1 evidence supports ambient geometry and ROS sensor transport in the Default Environment. ORE-MS has custom spectral and stochastic numerical models that could be coupled offline to Isaac-derived geometry. A controlled Isaac capture and that coupling both remain unqualified. ## Spectral attenuation feasibility

| Capability | Native or documented | Repository status and scientific use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wavelength-dependent transmission | RTX documents exponential fog and volumetric tint, density and scattering controls. | **Custom offline.** M02/M03 implement spectral coefficients and ordered slab transfer. No inspected bridge maps RTX color/fog parameters to an independently qualified coefficient in m⁻¹ or to an LWIR band. |
| Spatial variation | Renderer documentation includes height and density variation and non-uniform volumes. Availability in the deployed image was not checked. | **Custom offline.** M05's bounded advected field is sampled along a ray by M06. | Temporal stochasticity and replay | USD or renderer settings can represent visual changes. | **Custom offline.** M05 supplies seeded OU evolution; M06 stores realizations, path samples and detector seeds. **Absent** in AirStack as a qualified timed atmospheric capture schedule. | Independent transmission labels | Native camera depth or segment IDs tell where a ray hit. | **Absent natively for this integration.** Derive model-scoped truth with a separate analytic/numerical optical-depth integrator on the known path, using `Tλ = exp(−∫βext(λ,s,t) ds)`. Keep the raw model state and labels separate from observations. A separately implemented reference can test numerics. Its labels still depend on the assumed atmosphere. |

Use RTX fog for visualization if an approved future task needs it. The current documentation describes a visual renderer interface and does not qualify it as an ORE-MS transmission oracle. Extinction plus in-scattering means displayed brightness alone cannot identify direct-path transmission. ## M08 compatibility

The frozen code accepts the exact issue schema `case,realization,split,R_code,G_code,B_code,lwir_code`. It assumes one associated path and M07 detector codes under frozen response, illumination, nuisance and quantization settings. The synthetic range is 10 m, evaluated with 16 midpoint samples. The model uses its own stationary initialization and evolution, based on an advected 64 m periodic field. ROS image data, RGB8 transfer, unrelated model IDs and depth arrays do not supply those semantics. Preserve M08 and create a separate versioned adapter that admits only the supported path, time, detector and atmosphere bindings. Flags or prior fallback remain visible, as does hard abstention for unsupported paths or dynamics. [M08 plan](m08/EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md) · [Input contract](m08/DATA_CONTRACT.md) · [Prediction and guards](m08/predict.py)

| Arm | Required binding |
| --- | --- |
| Joint RGB/LWIR dynamic | Both modeled code sets on the same issue tick and path. Generate them using the admitted M07 likelihood/response assumptions and a separately documented observation generator. Real renderer pixels need independent radiometric calibration before they could replace those RGB codes. |
| RGB-only dynamic | Mask LWIR through the existing RGB mode; preserve the identical RGB observations, propagation, priors and reference labels. |
| Joint persistence | Use the same joint inputs and initial posterior. Carry the issue marginal to the same +2 s endpoint, retaining the configured fallback and abstention flags. |

Bind the scheduled issue tick to M08 time zero and record absolute simulation tick, issue availability, and planned label tick outside the CSV. The model has 0/2/6/18/48 s marginals, each 128 equally weighted burden samples. They aren't joint histories. Apply the unchanged monotone 550 nm map to each +2 s burden draw when the synthetic path contract matches. The result represents dimensionless **monochromatic 550 nm transmission**. It differs from broadband RGB, rendered image brightness and the physical M12 reference unless a separate band transfer is qualified. Keep ground-truth transmission at +2 s, analytic checks and environmental seeds sealed from the predictor until forecast lock. ## Minimum useful simulation experiment, proposed only

Use the **Default Environment** after a separately approved static fixture qualification. It has the best retained provenance, while Flat Plane and Simple Room remain alternate scene candidates. Add one fixed optical frame and opaque planar target with a surveyed central first hit at 10 m, stationary and unobstructed. Use a fixed simulation tick schedule and stable authored lighting. For the proposed capture contract, sample at 0.1 s intervals and place the label 20 sample intervals after issue, with integer simulation-tick associations. Use camera, calibration and depth captures to bind the geometry independently; issue-time spectral code generation stays offline. No PX4 action, moving platform, LiDAR dependence or online fog injection is needed. Prepare a separate, versioned fixture with a stage-local camera exporter or equivalent USD metadata export. Freeze stage assets, metre scale, up-axis, pose, intrinsics, camera convention, physics and render ticks, exposure settings, software and image digests. Establish the geometry with the M11 geometry procedure or a separately reviewed clear-path subset; the ambient default scene alone does not qualify the new target. [M11 fixture protocol](isaac-readiness/GEOMETRY_FIXTURE_PROTOCOL.md)

- **Process and reference.** Run one M05-compatible stochastic regime offline using seeds disjoint from the historical M08 holdout and prior bank. Generate RGB and LWIR codes from the same known path and issue state with a pinned M07-compatible forward adapter and separate detector noise. Independently integrate the stored latent path at the +2 s label tick for 550 nm transmission. Include a vacuum reference control with `T=1`, a constant-extinction closed-form control, and a frozen deterministic replay. A zero modeled burden with nonzero baseline extinction is a separate case from vacuum. Independently verify label convergence and declare numerical tolerance before outcomes.
- **Hypothesis and comparison.** Within this matched synthetic regime, joint dynamic conditioning lowers mean +2 s transmission CRPS versus both RGB dynamic and joint persistence. Propose one issue per realization, eight engineering-only development cases and 32 disjoint holdout realizations. Freeze that population and the seed before execution. For the two paired contrasts, use 10,000 realization-level bootstrap draws with seed 512026 and two-sided 97.5% percentile intervals. Pair by realization and keep all arms, reporting CRPS differences and intervals, median MAE, empirical 95% interval coverage and width, issuance and fallback. Freeze treatment of unavailable output and track coverage explicitly. A positive joint improvement whose paired intervals exclude zero against both arms supports the hypothesis in this fixture. Null or adverse differences stay in the result. The small sample can leave the comparison inconclusive. This is a synthetic decision rule, independent of the physical M12 threshold.
- **Artifacts and claim.** Retain stage and source manifests, camera and depth geometry receipts, issue and sealed reference tables, draw files, raw guard flags, and independently scored episode summaries. This tests the geometry-to-path binding and whether matched synthetic LWIR helps predict a known synthetic optical target. It cannot establish native Isaac spectral fidelity, physical LWIR performance, real aerosol calibration, detector or synchronization transfer to hardware, or M12 readiness. The frozen synthetic result should remain the comparator, and any numerical improvement is confined to this fixture.

**Recommended next task for review.** Qualify an **offline geometry-to-M08 issue adapter** on a frozen analytic one-ray fixture before requesting any Isaac launch. Dependencies are the governing pilot scope decision, the known 10 m/16-midpoint M08 contract, pinned M05–M08 inputs and the planned stage/clock manifest. Acceptance requires an independently checked 10 m first-hit and axis convention on the analytic input, an exact +2 s tick association, closed-form reference checks, admitted issue rows validated against `contract.py`, identical issue evidence across arms except LWIR masking, rejection of invalid path/time/rails per the directive, and byte-identical issue rows when future labels are replaced. The adapter remains separate from the frozen predictor. Any later Isaac fixture execution requires its own approved scene and geometry qualification.
