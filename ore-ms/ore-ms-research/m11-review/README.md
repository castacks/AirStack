## Scientific validity

- **Controls and leakage.** WP05 checks matching path geometry and foreground material binding. WP06 conditions on issue-time detector observations. Its future-only shift deliberately shares issue states, detector codes and novelty outputs with the matched branch. The M09 checkpoint is frozen and the issue call receives neither future state nor its regime label. WP07 generates fresh raster scenes and reuses frozen frontend, weights, calibration and cutoffs. The harness hands neutral metadata, codes and all-valid masks to inference. Fixture labels and target coordinates enter scoring after the decision. This is source-level evidence for separation. The per-trial ledgers needed to audit actual execution row by row were unavailable.
- **Randomization and estimands.** The challenge uses local fixed seeds and shared perturbations within each realization. The 48 scene clusters are the resampling units for 5,184 opportunities. A01 issued 647 correct and B03 661 out of 2,448 feasible opportunities. The independently computed difference is **−0.572 percentage points**. The submitted interval is **−3.082 to +1.622 points**. This review could not independently recompute it. At +3 px horizontal clear, A01 records 41/144 versus 0/144. At zero offset clear it records 48/144 versus 144/144. Those contrasts expose the fixture dependence. A01 issued on 397/2,736 negative scenes. Its Brier denominator (1,181) differs from B03's (1,992), so the candidate Brier values do not support a matched-population superiority claim. [Fusion evidence](../m11/evidence/fusion_checks.json)
- **Forecast uncertainty.** Recomputed nominal 95% coverage and Wilson intervals agree with the reported values. M07 matched and wrong-air counts were 186/192 and 92/192. M08 matched and future-shift two-second counts were 3,887/4,096 and 2,459/4,096. M09 corresponding counts were 242/256 and 162/256, with 7/256 novelty flags in each branch as reported. These are strong diagnostics of the prescribed shifts. Wilson intervals apply to per-regime rates and do not estimate a paired regime difference. CRPS uses different targets across M08's scalar latent check and M09's bounded burden check, so those numbers cannot rank the models against each other. The aggregate evidence does not allow individual CRPS or Brier rescoring. [Independent arithmetic](evidence.json) · [Submitted estimation comparison](../m11/ESTIMATION_FORECAST_COMPARISON.md)

**Conditions for disposition.** Preserve AC02, AC05, AC10, AC17, AC19 and AC21 at their existing gates. Keep the conditional M10 fixture result and negative cases together. For full numerical reproducibility of V09/V11, provide a checksummed raw decision CSV or paired scene counts and have a reviewer recompute the interval. Provide the directive attachment to the appointed reviewer if formal scope confirmation requires it. The Project Director decides the M11 disposition.
