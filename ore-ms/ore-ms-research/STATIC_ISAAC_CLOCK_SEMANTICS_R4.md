## Revised clock-only qualification

The standalone probe now preserves every delivered raw callback. It admits at most one epoch per bounded render step. Acceptance requires a strictly advanced physics-step index read from Isaac’s `World`, an advanced graph clock value, a matching publisher input, an increased publisher compute counter, and a live compatible Clock endpoint. The accepted timestamp must be present in the callbacks and must exactly match the pinned bridge’s integer encoding of the observed graph time. The probe samples physics and graph values before render, after return and after the callback wait. Evidence of further physics or graph-value changes during the wait invalidates the association. The physics index is an independent readback of physics callbacks. It does not imply that a render is exactly one physics tick. [Pinned physics-step accessor](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacSim/blob/v5.1.0/source/extensions/isaacsim.core.api/python/impl/simulation_context/simulation_context.py#L251) · [Physics counter update](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacSim/blob/v5.1.0/source/extensions/isaacsim.core.api/python/impl/simulation_context/simulation_context.py#L1419)

Identical copies are retained as repeats of their epoch. Queued copies of the previously accepted stamp may arrive before the new stamp. They cannot qualify the new step. Unexpected distinct stamps, a stalled clock or physics counter, incompatible or ambiguous publisher evidence, and a queue that still dispatches clock callbacks at the deadline leave the association unverified. Malformed or backward stamps fail. The bounded subscriber loop records the full set of callbacks it executes and the accepted timestamp separately. It does not claim a complete DDS queue history.

Only the clock-only diagnostic opts in. Geometry continues using its existing one-callback policy, matching native frame references, exact-time TF, the frozen receipt verifier, and exactly `2,000,000,000 ns` between the geometry observations. No clock values were rounded or substituted for geometry. A future decision to use clock epochs for geometry would require live qualification and compatible raw-to-receipt validation.
