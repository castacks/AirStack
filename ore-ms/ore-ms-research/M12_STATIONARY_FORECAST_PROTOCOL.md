# Proposed M12 stationary RGB/LWIR forecast transfer protocol

Planning only, 26 November 2026 UTC. Experiment family `ORE-MS-EXP-PER-001`. This proposal covers one physical transfer comparison within M12. It does not authorize equipment use, development capture, or holdout capture. [Roadmap reconciliation](ROADMAP_RECONCILIATION_2026-11-26.md) · [Governing mandate](RESEARCH_ROADMAP_MANDATE.md)

## Question and primary endpoint

Does a time-zero LWIR observation improve a two-second probabilistic forecast of **physical visible-band path transmission** beyond RGB-only forecasting, while retaining skill over a joint-observation persistence control?

- Forecast target is dimensionless band-weighted transmission `T_vis(t + 2 s)` along one fixed path. Measure it with an independent, calibrated, time-resolved transmission reference normalized to its clear-path response with background and source drift accounted for. Freeze the band weighting to match the model's RGB transmission definition or provide and qualify a fixed transfer. The reference label is independent of the two evaluation cameras and its calibration does not depend on their holdout outputs.
- Primary outcome is mean empirical CRPS in transmission units over physical attenuation episodes. For equal-weight draws, use mean absolute draw-to-reference distance minus half the mean absolute pairwise draw distance. Lower is better. Compare three paired arms on the same episode and issue epoch.
- **Hypothesis and proposed success gate.** The joint dynamic arm achieves at least 10% lower mean CRPS than each comparator and both paired improvement intervals exclude zero in its favor. This threshold is a proposed practical effect for the scoped pilot. It is not derived from the original synthetic score. A smaller or uncertain gain does not pass this gate.
- Secondary outcomes are forecast median MAE, nominal 95% interval coverage and width, forecast availability and guard reason, signed residuals in transmission units, and acquisition/scoring cost. Coverage and failure summaries are descriptive at this cohort size. The protocol does not test image localization, thermal crossover, future visibility, or navigation.

## Design and controls

| Arm | Issue-time observations | Future model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Joint dynamic | RGB and LWIR | M08-style conditional stochastic propagation |
| RGB dynamic | Same RGB sample, LWIR masked | Identical propagation, priors, path, support checks and output representation |
| Joint persistence | Same current joint posterior as joint dynamic | Carry its issue marginal forward unchanged |

Use the M08 classical architecture and its held-out controls as the starting point. Its frozen CLI and spectral/dynamics parameters are synthetic. A separately versioned and reviewed physical input adapter or qualified parameterization is necessary before capture can count as M12 evidence. Restrict changes to the physical observation and regime binding, estimated or checked on development only, and share those changes across applicable arms. If this binding cannot be demonstrated, stop. Do not substitute nominal simulation depth, digitized image brightness, degradation controller settings or the forecast's own inferred burden for reference transmission. [M08 model and controls](m08/SCIENTIFIC_MODEL.md) · [M08 data contract](m08/DATA_CONTRACT.md)

- **Conditions and variables.** Use one fixed RGB/LWIR bench rig, one surveyed path and radiometrically stable target, one separately approved attenuation regime, and fixed manual acquisition settings. Exposure condition varies between clear-path checks and independently reset stochastic attenuation episodes. The algorithmic factors are LWIR availability and propagation versus persistence. Keep target material, geometry, state of the optics, light and thermal settings fixed. Log ambient temperature, target and path-emission terms, gain/exposure, clipping, detector nonuniformity corrections, and drift.
- **Acquisition.** For each episode, collect a clear pre-control and a clear post-control with the same setup. Use one issue at a scheduled trigger-relative offset fixed in development, with a 2 s horizon. The control program, seed, attenuation reference, future camera frames and any privileged latent state stay outside predictor inputs. Independently reset and randomize disturbance realizations. Freeze the event order, trigger offset, session allocation and a capture window spanning both endpoints before the holdout. Retain all failed capture records.
- **Population.** Proposed cap is 12 development episodes and 24 held-out attenuation episodes, eight in each of three preassigned holdout sessions. Keep whole episodes and their controls together, with disjoint run IDs and randomization streams. Treat the episode as the replicate and report the result conditional on these sessions and the selected regime. Separate by session in resampling. Clear-path controls are diagnostic and excluded from the primary CRPS. This is a limited transfer test with a preselected effect threshold, with no claim of statistical power for smaller effects.
- **References and instrumentation.** Require a physical RGB camera and radiometrically calibrated LWIR camera, a common or independently calibrated clock, surveyed geometry and registered paths, stable reference sources, and an independent visible transmission instrument. Require independent characterization of the relevant LWIR response and path-emission/nuisance bounds for the predictor. If a companion LWIR path does not sample equivalent conditions, quantify the discrepancy before using it. Record reportable units, raw digital codes, the bandpasses, calibration validity range, and uncertainty budget. NISTIR 8098 informs checks for radiometric calibration, nonlinearity, drift and emissivity. Its high-magnification example does not qualify this rig or spectral band. [NISTIR 8098](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8098)
- **Reference qualification.** Proposed limits are pair exposure-midpoint separation and reference-to-target timestamp distance each at most 20 ms, with expanded visible transmission uncertainty at most 0.02 including timing, geometry/path mismatch, drift, and reference calibration. The clear controls must be within 0.02 of unity for each session reference position. Freeze measurement and guard rules after development, before held-out prediction. Flag rails, flat LWIR response and invalid path/reference explicitly. Lock expected temporal support at all issue times and scoring times before scoring any predictions.

## Scoring and decisions

Use the same held-out reference label for every arm. Freeze a development-only climatology as the scored fallback for any unavailable arm, retain the unavailable outcome and reason separately, and require native joint dynamic output on at least 22 of 24 valid episodes. This avoids comparing arms only where they issued. Every scheduled episode remains in the ledger. Treat missing or unqualified labels and failed preflight as inconclusive for the primary gate rather than selecting an easier subset. Report the valid subset descriptively when possible.

Score the two contrasts as comparator minus joint. Resample paired episodes within each predeclared session, with 10,000 draws and seed 12026. Use two-sided 97.5% percentile intervals for the two co-primary comparisons, conservatively controlling the familywise error rate at 5%. Report the observed scores and absolute and relative differences, per-session results, and a reference-uncertainty sensitivity using the declared low/high labels. Require both comparisons to maintain a positive point gain under those common low/high shifts. This sensitivity does not bound every possible reference-error combination. Preserve bias, interval width, empirical coverage, diagnostics on clear controls and all failed or withheld outputs.

- **No-go before collection.** No execution until M11 disposition and separate M12 authorization, a named physical lab and owner, reference instruments and calibrated RGB/LWIR access, an approved operating envelope, and a reviewable physical predictor plan exist.
- **No-go before holdout.** Development must qualify the reference and timing budgets, clear controls, sensor registration, spectral mapping and path-emission assumptions, and single-scattering/absorption validity for the claimed regime (AC05). Verify implementation of all three arms, counterfactual LWIR masking, delivery-time eligibility, and that changing future/reference files leaves forecasts identical. If any fails, preserve the failure and stop before holdout.
- **Go after scoring.** With a qualified full holdout and both CRPS criteria passed, advance only the claim that LWIR added two-second visible-transmission forecast skill in this one apparatus and regime. A qualified holdout that misses the gain, uncertainty or availability gate fails the proposed success condition. An invalid reference, incomplete required population or provenance failure is inconclusive. None of these outcomes automatically authorizes M13. Preserve null and negative results without alternate horizons, masks or thresholds.

## Reproducible execution and evidence handoff

1. At authorized setup, pin Git revision, physical adapter and scorer, rig/camera serials, reference certificates, clock calibration, spectral response, path geometry, environment and tolerances. Pin M08 reference source and configuration separately. The existing [M08 reproduction commands](m08/REPRODUCTION.md) check the synthetic model; they accept a synthetic input contract and do not exercise this physical design.
2. After the development checks, hash a run manifest with episode IDs, split, session, timing, settings and all frozen policies. Keep the randomization schedule with the capture operator, separate from the predictor. Use a run directory outside Git containing `manifest.json`, `capture/`, `issue/`, `reference/`, `locked_predictions/`, `scores/`, and `audit/`. Use immutable content hashes for each stage.
3. Each arm reads the same `issue.csv` with episode/session IDs, issue epoch, eligible camera measurements and frozen public metadata. Save the prediction table keyed by episode, horizon and arm, with draws, eligibility and fallback flags. Hash all prediction files before opening the separate reference table. An independent scorer joins one reference per episode at the prescribed horizon, audits units, timing and completeness, and writes CRPS, paired bootstrap, residual and calibration tables.
4. Run an independent replay against the locked input hashes. Retain calibration receipts, raw exposures, reference traces, prediction and score manifests, session/block and exclusion ledger, uncertainty budget, and a short pass/fail/inconclusive report. Keep raw data outside Git under the research evidence rules. Exact capture and physical adapter commands will be pinned during authorized apparatus qualification; this checkout contains no qualified physical runner or declared device inventory.
5. The existing stationary simulation may later be used for separately requested read-only checks of timestamp handling, recorder integrity and TF conventions. It cannot supply the LWIR or calibrated physical transmission labels needed here. No scene modification, degradation injection, hardware capture, numerical replay or runtime probe was executed for this planning review.
