# M04 frozen experiment and acceptance criteria

M04-R1 authorizes geometric and dynamic occlusion only. The [roadmap mandate](../RESEARCH_ROADMAP_MANDATE.md) and original research specification §§3.1, 3.2, 3.4 and 5–6 govern execution. The M01–M03 prerequisite decisions are conditional. Starting revision is `e7e0d86d25b26f394f155d32f054c29d99d3dc8e`. MATH-07 covers the camera transformation and projection derivatives, MATH-10 the visible-support reference and distinction from detectability, and INT-08 camera/scene/observation metadata.

Use a fixed pinhole, opaque world-axis-aligned rectangular patches on planes of constant world z, and deterministic linear translations sampled at the times in `scene.json`. Pixel centers have integer coordinates. The clear target projects to 7×5 centers in the baseline. Static closures block 1, 3, 5 and 7 of those columns. A far patch overlaps in image space and remains behind the target. Two nearer overlapping patches test distinct first hits. The moving foreground enters, crosses and leaves, and the moving target traverses the same static foreground and leaves the image. Freeze ordering, material controls, and settings in the configuration. Report range, camera depth, projected target membership, binary visible target and nearest surface for every pixel and frame. Denominator is in-frame projected target support before occlusion. An empty denominator is undefined.

Compare the unchanged M02 and M03 kernels on selected first surfaces. Use matched spectral/thermal material and an explicit distinct foreground, including a hidden-target material perturbation. The dark/thermally matched, atmospheric and detector-rail arms are geometry invariance controls. Direct surface terms use only first-hit material and slant range. Use a no-hit sentinel and no radiometry for rays outside all rectangles. Keep all results.

| Check | Frozen acceptance |
|---|---|
| V01 | 1e-10 absolute tolerance on known transformed coordinates, projected pixels and geometric range in their stated units. Analytic camera-frame projection Jacobian must agree with centered differences within 1e-7 px/m. |
| V02 | Zero label mismatches for near, far and overlapping obstacles. Permuting primitives must preserve every mask and first-hit label. Tie decisions must follow the declared priority and ID rule. |
| V03 | Exact in-frame projected support, first-hit and target-visible masks against an independent rational screen-space oracle for all frames. Maximum first-hit range/depth error ≤1e-10 m. Empty support retains an undefined fraction. |
| V04 | Exact configured time labels and rational reference visible counts at every sample. Verify at least one entry, crossing and exit, and changing support of the moving target. |
| V05 | Clear RGB band radiance vs analytic triangle area and flat-scene radiance within 2e-12 W·m⁻²·sr⁻¹. Clear LWIR band vs independent spectral Simpson within 5e-4 in the same units. Changing fully hidden target properties must give identical visible radiometry. In the attenuation control, check an analytic RGB layer and LWIR gray slab at the selected slant range with relative tolerance 2e-5. |
| V06 | Matched RGB/LWIR signals and masks agree for matched-material probe, and detector clamp and attenuation change observations while preserving all geometric labels. Zero mask mismatches over matched control pairs. |
| V07 | Closed rectangle edges included with no positional padding. Rays with absolute world z direction ≤1e-12 miss; path parameter <1e-8 m rejected. Tie group is within 1e-9 m of the actual nearest intersection, with foreground priority followed by stable lexicographic ID. Test boundary, near-parallel, exact/near tie, invalid or backward input. |
| V08 | Two fresh M04 executions reproduce all serialized outputs byte for byte. Check archived M01–M03 manifests and rerun their verification and comparison commands. The 13 milestones, five phases, 105 tasks and 147 edges remain unchanged. |

Absolute tolerances exceed double-precision roundoff for the bounded fixture. LWIR tolerances account for finite spectral quadrature. Binary projection does not approximate subpixel coverage. No perceptual detection rate is inferred.
