# M08 classical predictive distribution

M08 predicts the effective burden along a declared future 10 m longitudinal ray, and direct atmospheric transmission at 550 nm RGB and 11 µm LWIR. The targets are dimensionless. Transmission has an operational interpretation as the ratio of direct-beam radiance to independently calibrated incident radiance along that path. The events are RGB transmission below 0.55 and LWIR transmission below 0.88. Each band uses its own M05 coefficient. These events describe attenuation. Crossover, clipping, and geometric occlusion are separate controls, with no forecast of their frequency. Current detector noise enters only the M07 likelihood. Future process uncertainty enters the latent transition and is not added a second time as sensor noise.

The 5-dimensional field state is stationary Gaussian at initialization. For each coefficient, the exact OU transition over h seconds has conditional mean exp(−h/τ) times the initial coefficient and variance σ²(1−exp(−2h/τ)), with τ=6 s and σ=1 in the main model. The field basis is the unchanged M05 cosine/sine basis on a 64 m periodic domain. It advects at 1 m/s. Evaluate the nonlinear logistic physical map at 16 midpoint positions and average the results along the declared path. The scalar path mean is **insufficient**, as demonstrated by two reflected initial profiles with equal burden and markedly different future means. No Gaussian transition is applied directly to bounded burden. [State sufficiency and conditioning](STATE_SUFFICIENCY.md) · [Exact linear-SDE transition reference](https://users.aalto.fi/~asolin/sde-book/sde-book.pdf)

The M07 detector likelihood is reconstructed on its accepted grid, and the M05 prior ensemble is reweighted by that likelihood. Posterior field prior and uncertainty therefore include unobserved spatial degrees of freedom, and the h>0 ensemble also includes new OU innovations. The two detector likelihood contributions are conditionally independent only after conditioning on the shared burden and frozen nuisance inputs. The joint posterior conditions once on each admissible code and retains the shared latent field. Equal-weight quantile samples compress each empirical horizon marginal to 128 values. Relative likelihood interpolation, finite-bank size, finite spectral/path quadratures and quantile compression are numerical approximations. Fixed-bank scores are conditional on that sampled bank. The retained per-horizon marginals are not aligned joint path samples. Continuous density NLL is not reported for these discrete distributions. The fixed-bin smoothed log score is for the categorical burden target. [Proper-scoring reference](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf)

| Forecast | Issue and future uncertainty |
|---|---|
| Climatology | Empirical stationary distribution at h=0 repeated at every horizon. |
| Unconditioned physics | Same stationary initialization propagated through OU and advection. The exact stationary law agrees with climatology; finite banks differ. |
| Persistence | Holds the entire current conditioned burden distribution unchanged. It has no future process spread. |
| RGB, LWIR and joint | M07 admitted-channel likelihoods reweight common coherent latent trajectories. |
| Oracle | Privileged current latent coefficients, independent future innovations. This reference runs in a separate command. |
| Dynamics sensitivities | Same issue prior and likelihood with altered assumed τ=14 s or innovation scale σ=0.6. |

Current observation rejection produces a prior-only forecast on an independently specified target path where the environment model remains supported. Unknown target range/material or declared unsupported dynamics/domain trigger a hard abstention. No latent coefficient is supplied as operational truth. Unknown LWIR emission excludes the thermal channel. Model disagreement that is not supplied as a known guard can escape residual screening, as demonstrated by the retained wrong-air and wrong-dynamics cases. Real weather data, varying thermal air profiles, physical camera calibration and out-of-domain generalization remain unvalidated.
