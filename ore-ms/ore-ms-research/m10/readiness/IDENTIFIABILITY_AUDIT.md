# Identifiability audit

**Allowed environmental context** is range- and source-constrained path-average burden, conditional intervals and flags, and horizon-specific marginal reference-transmission distributions within declared matched assumptions. Current detector codes, radiometric measurements under known calibration, and independent geometric constraints can supplement context. None establishes task correctness or full state observability.

| Ambiguity | Existing test or evidence | Additional constraint needed |
|---|---|---|
| Range–extinction | M07 pairs 0.08 m⁻¹ × 12 m and 0.04 m⁻¹ × 24 m produce identical transmission. With two independently unknown band extinctions and unknown range, the scaled Jacobian has rank 2 of 3, null direction (−1,−1,+1). | Independent range, a justified spectral coupling, or independently constrained propagation geometry. Otherwise report optical-depth information only when illumination/source is constrained. [M07 structural audit](../../m07/OBSERVABILITY.md) |
| Unknown source and thermal emissivity–temperature | M07 produces equal observations under different source/attenuation and emissivity/temperature combinations. Unknown layered emission invalidated the ordinary M06 LWIR burden inverse. | Independent radiance/source or material constraints, a temperature/profile observation, or a validated restricted transfer model. Without it, withhold the affected inverse. [M07 holdout evidence](../../m07/HOLDOUT_ANALYSIS.md) |
| Path-average burden versus latent field | Two M08 reflected fields start at the same average burden, 0.5. Their 6 s predictive means differ by 0.164 under paired innovations. The nonlinear average loses phase information. | Independent spatial/time-resolved observations and a qualified observability analysis for the wider state. Until then use model-conditional marginalized field distributions, never point-valued recovered coefficients. [M08 sufficiency audit](../../m08/STATE_SUFFICIENCY.md) |
| Shared spectral dependence | M06 reference transmission correlation is 0.9922 on fixed path, with the cyclic other-seed comparator at −0.0628. M07 joint information increased in a matched scalar fixture while rank stayed 1. | Conditional residual and joint task-error calibration by realization. Neither independent environmental evidence nor independent task errors follows from two channels. [M06 joint analysis](../../m06/JOINT_ANALYSIS.md) |
| Visibility and future dynamics | M04 matched foreground and target can yield identical codes with different first hits. M09 matched and shifted dynamics produce identical issue codes/forecasts. | Independent visibility/association evidence or time-resolved process checks as appropriate. Where the distinction is absent, retain ambiguity or restrict the claim. [M04 first hits](../../m04/ACCEPTANCE_REPORT.md) · [M09 robustness](../../m09/ROBUSTNESS.md) |

AC10 remains open. Joint observation can improve one constrained estimand in some regimes, but does not close any of these ambiguities. A network interval or novelty score cannot substitute for missing independent information.
