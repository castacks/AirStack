# Inference-time information

The classification below applies to the proposed bounded synthetic experiment. Physical assets require their own independently qualified records. Admission is stamped with acquisition time, frame ID, provenance, validity state and uncertainty where applicable. Missing inputs are explicit. The scoring side holds privileged truth in a separate artifact.

| Quantity | Classification | Provenance and uncertainty | Admissible use |
|---|---|---|---|
| RGB detector measurements | Directly observed | Timestamped integer RGB codes, exposure/gain settings, missing/invalid status and rail flags. Noise and quantization remain. | Current task frontend, integrity checks and validated observation model. |
| LWIR detector measurements | Directly observed | Timestamped LWIR codes with known synthetic detector settings, quantization and rail flags. Codes can be equal at crossover. | Current task frontend and qualified inverse likelihood. |
| Intrinsics, extrinsics, time alignment and response | Independently constrained in the synthetic fixture | Versioned camera and detector metadata, known coincident projection and synthetic clock. Physical registration, response and synchronization remain open. | Admissibility preflight and coordinate transforms. Independently verify any perturbation handling. |
| Target search geometry and range | Independently constrained when supplied, otherwise unavailable | Search region and permitted range or interval from independent fixture metadata, without target position, presence or first-hit truth. | Condition an inverse only inside its verified range domain. A search region or range does not certify association or visibility. |
| Reflectance, emissivity, temperature of target and background, illumination or reflected/path source | Independently constrained in named controls, otherwise unavailable | Material/scene contract with declared tolerances or independent measurements. Unknown quantities retain an explicit unknown status. | Qualify only the applicable estimate. No imputation of simulator values. |
| Effective path-average burden | Estimated | M07 scalar in [0,1], conditional on admitted detector codes, known association and independently constrained range/material/source. Weak/mismatch/abstain flags are part of its interface. | Context feature within matched validity, with posterior and flags attached. |
| Actual M05 Fourier coefficients and physical burden | Privileged simulator truth | True latent states, atmosphere layers, and exact line integrals from generator. | Scoring and separate diagnostic oracle. Latent draws used internally by M08 are model samples, not observations. |
| M07 posterior or prior fallback | Estimated | Conditional posterior and diagnostics from the frozen issue-only inverse. The uniform prior is a labeled comparator. | Conditional environmental context, subject to AC10/AC17. No direct mapping to class/location confidence. |
| M08 forecast marginals | Estimated | Distribution of future burden and fixed-path reference transmissions, with horizon, model identity and fallback status. Process parameters are externally declared in verified cases. | Candidate short-horizon context under matched priors and dynamics. Never a prediction of geometric visibility. |
| M09 forecast marginals and novelty score | Estimated | Learned, horizon-wise conditional distributions. Novelty measures issue-code atypicality, with missed shifts and false alarms. | Optional experimental feature and warning. A quiet novelty score does not clear an unsupported condition. |
| Thermal path emission and air temperature | Independently constrained in known-air controls, otherwise unavailable | Uniform declared air is a qualified synthetic input. General layered source terms are not inferred from a single LWIR band. | Admit LWIR-derived burden only under the corresponding constraint. Direct LWIR task evidence can be evaluated separately for the supported hardware/scene regime. |
| Occlusion or target association status | Independently constrained only when a separate legitimate sensor or fixed experimental eligibility constraint supplies it. Otherwise estimated or unavailable. | Any evidence source needs frame identity, latency and error characterization. RGB/LWIR codes do not uniquely distinguish target absence, matched foreground or crossover. | Validated eligibility or uncertainty cue. Simulator first-hit labels are withheld. |
| Saturation indicators | Directly observed for recorded hardware flags or known rails | Rail-valued codes are observable, and distinguish only censored readings. Actual pre-rail radiance remains unknown. | Mask affected evidence and account for remaining support. Never invert clipped codes as exact radiance. |
| Ground-truth projected support, visibility, first hit, task labels and future states | Privileged simulator truth | Renderer labels, full environmental realization and post-issue observations. | Scoring, diagnostics and explicitly separated oracle only. |
| Future detector measurements and future target visibility at issue | Unavailable | Not yet acquired, even if stored in the experiment dataset. | Scoring only after the decision is logged. |

The future implementation must enforce an input allowlist, per-frame/horizon time cutoff, and independent context and evaluation stores. Identical allowed inputs and context must give identical outputs for any fixed policy state and declared random seed. Mode labels may separate evaluation arms but may not reveal hidden intervention identity.
