# M10 scientific readiness report

**Decision: READY FOR M10 WITH CONDITIONS.** The accepted work supports a bounded synthetic experiment in visible-target localization. Existing environmental posteriors, transmission forecasts and novelty scores do not yet establish calibrated task reliability. They may be tested as candidate context and must be compared with independently calibrated fixed and single-modality references.

## Implementation blockers and minimum preflight

1. **Authorization.** Obtain a separate Project Director directive to implement M10. AC20 remains open for implementation authorization.
2. **Task benchmark.** Before policy work, establish the frozen corpus, lineage/splits, independent label and timestamp audit, functioning RGB/LWIR/fixed comparators, and disjoint reliability calibration. A successful preflight requires checksummed populations and evidence from the synthetic support, absence and occlusion fixtures.
3. **Input eligibility and scope.** Enforce the issue-time allowlist, independent nuisance/association constraints, synthetic registration, and explicit fallback. Unknown source/range, misspecified process and blind shift block reliance on affected adaptive context in those cells. Limit the main experiment to matched synthetic conditions and treat such scenarios as controls until additional closure evidence exists.
4. **Weight calibration.** A completed experiment must measure correctness and conditional error coupling at matched coverage. Existing intervals and OOD scores cannot by themselves authorize a weight or support a perception claim.

No AC10, AC17, AC19, AC21, AC05 or AC06 closure is recommended. M11 independent simulator verification, M12 physical validation/registration, and organizationally independent assurance remain at their assigned gates. No fusion policy was implemented, trained or evaluated. M10 remains pending and not started.
