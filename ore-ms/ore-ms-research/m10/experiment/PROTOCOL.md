# M10-R1 prospective protocol

The archived PF1 population and B01–B03 outputs are the experimental foundation. The primary A01 policy uses current candidate and detector evidence. Its primary contrast is A01 − B03 in correct supported localizations per feasible holdout opportunity. A gain claim requires a positive lower endpoint from the 10,000-draw paired realization bootstrap, the two absent/hidden Wilson upper endpoints at or below 5%, and an improvement at matched coverage. Matched coverage uses the prespecified 50%, 60%, 65% and 70% of all opportunities. Use fractional allocation across exact score ties for descriptive curves and report insufficient candidate coverage. Keep all failed operating cells. No performance across unmatched or unsafe strata is implied. All variants, other contrasts and strata are secondary with pointwise intervals.

## Models

B04 uses two constant correctness frequencies fit on development candidates to combine associated single-modality candidate centroids. A01 fits a separate ridge logistic regressor for each modality on development, with an intercept and four bounded features. Use the frozen raw contrast score, normalized log component size, valid-pixel fraction and rail-pixel fraction. Map each feature from [0,1] to [−1,1]. Positive training outcomes require feasible truth and a candidate within 1 px. Include candidates on negatives in the fit. A realization is the uncertainty unit. Each group contributes its observed candidate rows. The optimizer uses deterministic Newton steps with fixed regularization and convergence checks. Estimate development residual common failure relative to the product of empirical marginal failures separately for associated and conflicting pairs. Shrink it by n/(n+64). For associated candidates add the residual to the product of modeled failure probabilities and clamp to the Fréchet bounds. Use one minus this adjusted failure as a ranking statistic which still requires separate task calibration.

Join candidates when their centroids are within 2 px. A01 combines them by convex interpolation using the modality task scores, with each contribution bounded between 20% and 80%. B04 uses fixed weights in the same bounds and abstains on conflicts. On conflict A01 selects a single candidate only if its modeled probability is at least 0.65 and exceeds the other by at least 0.20. Record the conflict warning separately from the resulting decision. Otherwise withhold a candidate. A single eligible candidate follows its modality route and still needs the arm's calibrated issuance threshold. Missing both abstains. The selection model never receives a realized label, data order, or join key.

A07 removes environmental context and is mathematically identical to primary E03. A08 removes learned correctness inputs, substituting equal contributions and the frozen candidate contrast; it keeps the same geometry, qualification and fallback framework. A09 uses the A01 position mechanism and explicit conditional-independence ranking on associated pairs. E04–E06 are attempted under the unchanged observational contract. An unavailable environmental input leaves the E03 proposal unchanged and is reported as an unsupported contextual contrast. Candidate scores are ranking statistics until a separate calibration-only mapping is applied.

## Calibration and scoring

Use ten equal-width bins and the smoothed event frequency (hits + 1)/(candidates + 2) for each new arm, including candidates on infeasible cases. A bin with zero calibration support supplies no qualified forecast. Choose a threshold on calibration by highest correct count subject to the frozen absent and fully hidden Wilson limits separately, then fewer primary false-supported cases, then the higher threshold. Require useful correct issuance. Use 1.01 when no threshold qualifies. Historical B01–B03 mappings and thresholds remain frozen.

The oracle uses only the candidate positions proposed by B01–B04 and primary A01 on each opportunity. It issues a correct candidate when one exists. Also report a matched issued-opportunity subset using B03's exact issue mask. These two diagnostic ceilings have different opportunity constraints and use privileged correctness in scoring only.

Scoring logs correct, issued, feasible, Brier, weighted absolute bin calibration error, within-feasible location error, safety, fallback, pair correctness, and negative transfer. For the conflict-warning audit, define degradation as a wrong or missing modality candidate with an alternative correct candidate on a feasible opportunity; track warning misses and false warnings conditional on two eligible sensors. Those errors concern task evidence and do not diagnose the underlying process. Record context admissions, missing context frequency and blind-future equivalence separately. Use descriptive Wilson upper endpoints within negative strata and grouped resampling for paired contrasts and repeated controls.

## Frozen-stage procedure

1. Replay and verify the stored PF1 observations, labels, candidates and holdout summary before adaptive implementation.
2. Materialize only development observations and labels for the fit. Run proposals in a separate restricted filesystem process. Materialize calibration after fitting, run unchanged proposals and create the calibration receipt.
3. Freeze protocol, model, source, calibration, fallback and evaluation hashes before materializing any holdout observations for adaptive inference. Open holdout truth for scoring after proposals exist. The historical PF1 replay is an integrity check and does not supply inputs for model fitting.
