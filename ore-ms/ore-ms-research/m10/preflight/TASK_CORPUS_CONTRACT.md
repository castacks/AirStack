# Frozen task corpus contract

Common camera centers are zero-based (x column, y row), with +x right, +y down and +z forward. A 20 px focal length and center (16,12) define the 33×25 shared ideal grid. Time is integer milliseconds relative to scene start. Nominal acquisition equals 2000 ms. A stale control reports 1900 ms and is excluded from the affected modality. Known offset prevents registered joint use, and undeclared offset remains a blind failure control. Camera projection and all units follow the frozen config.

`inference/frames.csv` contains opaque opportunity ID, split, independent range if supplied, material contract status, sensor timestamps and qualification/registration metadata. `codes.npy` stores four integer bands (RGB then LWIR) and `masks.npy` stores per-band 0 unavailable, 1 valid interior and 2 rail. IDs and split are joins, never score features. Inputs are current acquisitions and independent constraints. No true range substitutes for missing metadata. No interpolation, re-registration or geometry correction consumes scorer truth.

`scoring/labels.csv` contains opaque ID, realization, intervention, projected and target-first counts, visibility fraction, centroid, feasibility and distinct negative class. Blank centroid means no visible target; blank fraction means zero projected support. `scoring/geometry.npy` separates target projection and first-hit identity with codes 0 background, 1 target, 2 foreground, 3 distractor. `scoring/scene.csv` records the geometric recipe, physical state and future-only controls. Inference reads none of these. Exact opaque joins rather than latent scene labels identify corresponding arrays. The scorer checks all row and ID coverage.

Codes are deterministic seeded samples of synthetic RGB and LWIR. Noise and clipping are distinct from process evolution. Legacy forward transfer is reused through an equivalent homogeneous-layer spectral calculation, qualified against M06 on separate probes. Each high-level layer control is spatially homogeneous by design, whereas slant lengths are per pixel. Calibrated hardware inference and environmental forecast context are outside PF1.
