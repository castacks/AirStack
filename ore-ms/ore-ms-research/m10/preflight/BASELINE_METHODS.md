# Frozen nonadaptive baseline methods

B01 forms mean normalized RGB code on pixels with all three channels valid. B02 uses normalized LWIR. For each eligible modality, subtract the valid-pixel image median and clamp negative differences to zero. This is a controlled positive-contrast frontend with no task probability interpretation. Threshold maps at the detector-specific raw contrast in configuration. Extract the four-connected components with at least four supported pixels, return the candidate centroid and its peak raw excess above the image median. Pick the greatest score and break ties by first flattened pixel index. The search is the common full frame. Metadata excludes stale, invalid and flagged unregistered thermal samples before comparing.

B03 normalizes the two positive contrast maps by their frozen minimum contrasts. If both have candidates, require their centroids within 2 px and average the normalized maps equally on shared valid pixels. Apply a unit threshold and identical component and centroid rules. The reported raw ranking score is the mean normalized-map candidate score divided by four and capped at one, matching the normalized score convention for each single arm. If only one candidate is eligible, propagate that candidate as an explicit single-modality fallback under the same qualification and calibration rules. Candidate conflict abstains. No environmental variable, confidence weight or learned gate enters fusion. The fixed comparator has its own calibration mapping. No truth-derived association is used.

Raw scores rank detector contrast only. Locate visible support rather than predicting physical occupancy. An unavailable, unsupported or low-contrast input may return no candidate. An independent scoring stage later applies a calibration-only threshold to qualify supported issuance.
