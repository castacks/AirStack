# M07 data access contract

M07-R1 conditionally accepts M06 and completes Phase II. AC14 closed before implementation. AC10 remains open for joint unknown range/extinction or latent-driver recovery. The accepted claim can be narrowed to effective burden with independently fixed nuisance constraints. See the [live register](../ACCEPTANCE_CONDITIONS_REGISTER.md) and [roadmap](../RESEARCH_ROADMAP_MANDATE.md).

| Information class | Allowed use |
|---|---|
| Inference | Strict feature CSV with corpus, case ID, realization key, split, elapsed time, and four integer detector codes. Model settings, independently supplied range and association, reflectance/emissivity/temperature, response, scattering and path-source constraints come only from the frozen fixture registry. IDs select a declared fixture and join outputs. They never encode a scene state or drive the likelihood. |
| Evaluation only | Separate label CSV with burden. M06 latent coefficients, path temperatures, first-hit labels, true range, true optical depths, analog/noiseless radiance, and true transmission are forbidden as estimator inputs. The scoring process opens labels after inference. |
| Oracle | Analytical inversion of a deliberately supplied exact monochromatic transmission with known range. This uses an independent privileged synthetic quantity and is reported only as a limiting control. The main tables never pass transmission to inference. |

The M06 audit verified 96 development and 32 holdout realizations, both geometry arms at 0/6/12 s, exact detector settings in qualified metadata, and available 12-bit detector codes. Its RGB coefficients share a wavelength law and constant single-scattering albedo and illumination, so under the documented synthetic nuisance constraints the band depends on integrated burden even for heterogeneous paths. The fixed-arm target association and 10 m center-ray length are independent fixture inputs and do not come from M06 first-hit columns. The covered arm is a known blocked geometry fixture. Its detector values may contain foreground but are never treated as target measurements. LWIR path self-emission depends on unknown M06 layer temperatures. Ordinary M06 LWIR therefore abstains, and the joint adapter admits RGB only. The fixed known-air M07 corpus provides a genuinely admissible paired inverse experiment.

Both controlled and M06 partitions preserve whole realizations across views, sensors and timestamps. Thresholds and model constants are frozen before outcomes. No fitted model or tuning is performed on either partition. The controlled corpus uses synthetic known nuisance constraints, with named arms withholding or violating them. Rail and invalid codes are recognized through the measurement channel, independently of the source corpus clip labels. Leakage checks mutate forbidden fields and require invariant feature projection and likelihood inputs. The estimator rejects unexpected columns. No training, sequential filtering, future state prediction or fusion policy is authorized.
