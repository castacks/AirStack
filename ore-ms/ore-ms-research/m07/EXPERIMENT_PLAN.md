# M07 frozen protocol

Starting revision bfb26be02. Original specification §§3.1, 3.4, 5–6 and [M07 governed scope](../RESEARCH_ROADMAP_MANDATE.md) apply. MATH-15/16 and INT-13 cover observability, likelihood, and inverse replay. MATH-17–20 sequential methods remain unexecuted. The [data contract](DATA_CONTRACT.md), M06 archive, public `config.json` and separate `generation.json` are locked before final evaluation. The M06 probe partitions are reused at 0/6/12 s. The new controlled calibration set uses 384 independent uniform-state realizations, split 128/256 by seed index. Cases reuse state and noise seeds within each realization. Evaluate all arms. Compare a uniform prior, RGB-only, LWIR-only, and joint likelihood on the same observations. Score all outputs and report failures, abstentions, conditional 95% interval coverage, and aggregate errors. Use only the matched noisy arm to judge frequentist coverage under the declared prior/noise experiment. Test actual M06 independently as a noiseless transfer replay, with no calibrated-coverage claim for it.

The scalar target is effective dimensionless burden, with physically declared [0,1] bounds. Use a uniform 401-cell grid and independent Gaussian radiance-domain likelihood applied to decoded nonrail codes, combining read/thermal noise and LSB/√12 in quadrature. Censor by abstention/omission at rails. The requested data classes are separate files and only features plus the externally constrained registry enter the estimator. Use posterior means and equal-tailed 95% intervals. Flat channels with endpoint change ≤1e−10 W·m⁻²·sr⁻¹ are omitted, posterior SD ≥0.24 or local whitened sensitivity <1 is flagged weak, and minimum fitted maximum standardized residual >5 is a mismatch warning. Unknown range, source/material, association, or unavailable air constraints disable the affected inverse instead of filling them with truth. No sequential filter or forecast.
