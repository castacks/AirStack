# M07 conditional environmental inverse

The sole estimated variable is path-average optical burden b in [0,1], dimensionless. This is M05's effective field integrated along a known target ray. RGB extinction at 550 nm is k₀+a b in m⁻¹. The scattering fraction and wavelength exponent determine RGB spectral coefficients. LWIR absorption at 8/11/14 µm uses separate affine functions of b in m⁻¹. At fixed length, these coefficients imply optical depth Lκ. Neither b nor the coefficients identify physical aerosol composition. Range, material properties, illumination/path source, sensor settings, and target association are independently constrained for each admissible synthetic setup. LWIR additionally needs constrained path self-emission, supplied by a known uniform air temperature in the new experiment. Unknown nuisances are excluded under the tested contract. No separate temperature, range, material, or latent-field recovery is claimed.

For a known RGB material, subtracting the constant path source at each wavelength gives the incident contrast multiplied by transmission. M06's constant scattering fraction/illumination makes the distributed path equivalent to one optical depth even though coefficients vary spatially. This follows by repeated composition with a common fixed point. LWIR's source varies with unknown path temperature in M06 and has no such guaranteed reduction. On the controlled known-air corpus the same conditional affine form holds for thermal transfer, with its own spectral absorption and emissivity/reflection. The forward code calls the unchanged M06 adapter and accepted M02/M03 spectral physics. M06 optical-depth truth is never read at inference.

Posterior weights on 401 uniform cells are proportional to exp(−χ²/2) with a uniform prior. The likelihood uses decoded integer codes with additive independent Gaussian detector noise, plus LSB/√12 in quadrature to approximate quantization. Rails are omitted and a flat or uncalibrated channel contributes no information. The omission is an abstention convention and does not model censoring likelihood. Matched calibration uses nominal nonsaturated conditions. Modes use RGB, LWIR, both supported channels, or the prior alone. Report a posterior mean, standard deviation, equal-tailed 95% interval by within-cell interpolation, and local scalar information from a grid derivative. Rank and null directions are analyzed separately. A bounded prior can create finite bounds even if the observation is uninformative, so hard abstentions leave inference summaries blank. A weak or mismatch conditional posterior remains visible with its warning and does not establish valid identification. [Bayesian inverse formulation](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0962492910000061)

Local information sums only independent conditional detector contributions. A second modality can improve precision about b while failing to add rank or resolve nuisance ambiguity. When source/temperature models are wrong, even narrow intervals and low fitted residuals can be misleading. The mismatched-air control demonstrates this. Known and uncertain nuisance inputs are paired by realization, and missing and saturated cases retain their diagnostic outputs. State priors and physical assumptions are documented separately from measurement noise. Each frame is estimated independently. No transition model, smoothing, forecasting, learning or fusion policy is implemented.
