# Geometry fixture protocol

Use the frozen M11 WP03 setup. In optical coordinates, the 33 × 25 pinhole camera has fx = fy = 20, cx = 16 and cy = 12, with integer pixel-center sample indices. Use metres and declare the USD-to-ROS optical-frame transform explicitly. Keep the target centered at (0, 0, 10) with half-extents (2, 2). Place the opaque blocker at (-2.83 + t, 0, 5) with half-extents (0.81, 1.13) at times 0, 2 and 5 s. The three analytical cases cover 2,475 rays. Freeze [expected pixel IDs, axial depths and ray ranges](evidence/fixture-reference.json) before capture. A null depth indicates no hit. Treat exact edge or equal-depth ties separately and preserve them in the evidence.

Apply the M11 draft pilot tolerances without tuning: exact non-boundary first-hit IDs, projected control points within 0.1 px, ray-range error at most 1 mm and exact paired stamps. For axial depth, multiply by the pinhole ray norm before comparing ray range. Use an integer tick schedule, identity and a separate nonidentity pose control, static and moving blocker intervals, and two independent image/stage-pinned captures. Record edge discrepancies instead of hiding them with filtering. Pin stage assets, renderer, SDG seed, time step, and software before capture. Score numerical and ROS data independently and record Foxglove display observations separately.
