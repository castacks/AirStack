# Synthetic dataset qualification

**Disposition** PASS for the frozen synthetic configuration. The corpus qualifies for controlled simulation analysis under the declared scene and coefficient distribution. Physical registration, empirical weather coverage and real-hardware validity remain open.

Six layouts contribute 25 full 9×7 frames across 15 control cases. The two center-probe ensemble arms contribute 768 frames from 128 independent seeds at three times. Both arms inherit each seed's development or holdout designation, giving 576 development and 192 holdout probe frames. The probes are counted separately from full images. The full corpus has 793 frames, 2,343 rows, 2,280 valid paired observations, 63 no-hit rows, 1,205 unique finite paths, and 17,000 segment rows. No duplicate record keys or unreferenced paths were retained.

The verifier checks frame/pair/path joins, exact case/time/grid coverage, units and kernel/configuration checksums, masks against a separate front-parallel projection calculation, first-hit range and material, segment endpoints and ground truth, independent detector rail and quantization equations, and finite required values. Three injected corruptions (duplicate row, nonfinite direction and inconsistent environment key) were rejected. Empty support leaves visibility fraction blank, full occlusion has a numeric zero, and clipped values retain both the code and rail flags. Atmospheric controls cover both isolated spectral families, heterogeneous time-varying transfer, deterministic motion and combinations. Marginal and joint diagnostics characterize the specified synthetic population. No tuning or training used the designated holdout.
