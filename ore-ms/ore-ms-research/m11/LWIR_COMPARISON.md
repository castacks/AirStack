# LWIR radiative transfer

Twelve Planck comparisons span 8/11/14 µm and temperatures 220–330 K. The benchmark evaluates the frequency-domain law and converts via the spectral Jacobian to W m⁻² sr⁻¹ µm⁻¹. Maximum relative discrepancy was 1.74e-15. Two slabs separately exercise attenuated emission, attenuated reflected background, and self-emission under LTE. Their independent integrals are 12.34793, 2.15032 and 19.61156 W m⁻² sr⁻¹. With 12/24/48/96 trapezoid intervals the maximum term errors were 3.159e-3 / 7.903e-4 / 1.976e-4 / 4.940e-5 W m⁻² sr⁻¹. Final error ratio was 0.250, consistent with second-order convergence on knot-aligned grids. Finest relative term errors were about 4.00e-6, 1.78e-5 and 6.67e-8.

The sign reversed through thermal crossover, including ±1e-9 K perturbations. At exact crossover the two implementations both returned zero. The largest crossover residual was below 4e-15 W m⁻² sr⁻¹ µm⁻¹. The 12-bit detector agreed at below-range, both exact bounds, an interior point and above-range. LWIR uses strict overflow flags.
