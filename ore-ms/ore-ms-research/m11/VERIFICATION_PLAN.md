# M11-R1 prospective verification plan

Subject revision: `ff64941152ad10b0656a2c68d80eb4121b070b97`. The comparisons below use new fixtures. Historical M01–M10 paths remain immutable except the explicitly live governance files. No pilot or physical execution is in scope.

| Work | Prospective population and criterion |
|---|---|
| WP01 | RGB two slabs, 450/550/650 nm, 0/0.2/1/4 optical depth per slab. Integrate the formal source with QUADPACK. Absolute tolerance 1e-11 in transmission and spectral radiance. Detector 20,000 draws at 50 and 200 electrons, mean within 5 standard errors plus half LSB, variance within 8% after decoding. Compare clear, zero illumination, and rails. An energy-conserving isotropic two-stream slab is an approximation challenge at optical depth 0.1/1/4, with no physical-agreement gate. |
| WP02 | Planck 8/11/14 µm at 220/290/300/330 K. Relative tolerance 1e-12. Weighted band 12/24/48/96 intervals with a response knot at 11 µm. Finest absolute tolerance 0.005 W m⁻² sr⁻¹ and final refinement ratio below 0.30. Compare emissive and reflected terms through two slabs, crossover absolute 1e-10 W m⁻² sr⁻¹, and exact detector codes away from ambiguous rounding ties. |
| WP03 | Pixel-center projection and first-hit range on a 33×25 grid at three occluder times. Independent homogeneous projection and projected planar polygons. All IDs exact and distances within 1e-10 m. Edge ties use an explicit inclusive convention. Registration sweeps in WP07. |
| WP04 | Four step lengths including 1e-8 s, stationary SD 1.1, tau 6 s. Exact transition via matrix exponential and variance integral, tolerance 1e-12 absolute. At least 20,000 independent trajectories for mean, variance and advected covariance, errors within 0.04 standardized units. Euler step halving must reduce variance bias. Check finite 10,000-step paths and deterministic clear/degraded/clear ordering. |
| WP05 | M06 vacuum, homogeneous dual and advected cases, plus foreground/background range challenge. Independent continuous spectral integrals and piecewise-ray source integration. Matched-path total per channel tolerance max(0.005, 0.003 × reference), with segment refinement 4/8/16/32 reported separately. Isothermal LTE and coefficient limits are dimensional controls. |
| WP06 | M07 continuous bounded posterior for fixed synthetic interior code controls, using a separate spectral model and normalized quadrature. Mean and endpoints within 0.015 in burden. Independent matched and wrong-air interval checks use at least 128 realizations. Show a same-depth range/extinction ambiguity. M08 check exact Gaussian horizon covariance against the unchanged transition and fixed-ray projection, tolerance 1e-11 at matched inputs. Score matched, wrong tau, wrong amplitude, and future-only shifts using 4,096 independent scalar coefficients. Calibration under shift is descriptive. Quantify parameter variation separately from conditional process variance. M09 historical scores receive provenance-only review unless a qualified independent issue-input population can be executed. |
| WP07 | Cross 48 independent scene realizations with target size 2/4/6 pixels, axis and diagonal offsets of nominal size 0/1/2/3/4, and clear/edge/partial/distractor controls. No recalibration. Threshold sensitivity is diagnostic only at 1, 2 and 3 px. Score fixed B01–B04/A01 on fresh raster detector arrays through the approved feature contract. Use realized scene as the paired bootstrap cluster, 2,000 draws. Report fixture strata and candidate calibration separately, with no pooled transfer claim to PF1. |

Statistical seeds are M11-local and independent of M01–M10 seeds. No comparison is admitted without a frozen mapping, units and an applicable criterion or explicit descriptive disposition. A directional change can be conclusive without establishing a physically valid tolerance. CPU timings use phase boundaries and per-scene latency and are excluded from deterministic evidence hashes.
