# Environmental estimation and forecasting

**M07.** The independent 4,001-point posterior density integrates a separately computed LWIR spectral model against the same frozen interior-code likelihood and uniform prior. Across 192 matched and 192 wrong-air draws, posterior means and endpoints agree within 3.67e-5 in burden. Matched coverage was 96.88% (95% Wilson 93.35–98.56%). A 15 K air mismatch reduced coverage to 47.92% (40.96–54.95%), narrowed mean interval width from 0.613 to 0.478, and raised mean CRPS from 0.087 to 0.270. All wrong-air trials remained classified informed. The shared likelihood cannot distinguish three path-length/extinction pairs with common depth 0.6 and transmission 0.5488. Known range and conditional LWIR nuisance inputs are essential to this comparison. [Posterior data](evidence/posterior_checks.json)

**M08.** For a fixed state the independently integrated OU covariance and ray-burden projection agree to 2.22e-16 and 1.11e-16 respectively. In 4,096 scalar forecast trajectories, nominal 95% coverage at 2 s was 94.9% matched, 99.2% under slow truth dynamics, 100.0% rounded with low innovation amplitude and 60.0% under future-only shift. The independent Gaussian CRPS under matched/shifted truth was 0.395/1.020 at 2 s and 0.562/1.004 at 48 s. At initial state 1 and 2 s, mean aleatoric variance was 0.4957 and between-parameter conditional-mean variance was 0.00751 for the declared nine-point sensitivity prior. The fixed-state and scalar tests address dynamics and scores without independently re-executing the full M08 initialization bank. [Classical data](evidence/forecast_checks.json)

**M09.** The frozen physics-768-901 checkpoint issued on 256 fresh, independently simulated inputs in each regime. The future-only shift reused the exact issue outputs, including novelty flags. Matched burden coverage at 2 s was 94.5% with CRPS 0.078. The future-only shift reduced coverage to 63.3% and raised CRPS to 0.168. The checkpoint flagged 7/256 cases in both regimes, so the detector-side novelty score did not signal the future-only mechanism change. Wrong air raised flags to 43/256 and reduced issue-time coverage to 85.5% versus 94.5% in the matched case. Slow dynamics and reduced innovation variance produced overcoverage at some horizons. This is a transfer check for one checkpoint and the burden target. Training-seed and other-target robustness remain unverified. [Learned forecast data](evidence/learned_checks.json)
