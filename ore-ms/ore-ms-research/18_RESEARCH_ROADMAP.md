# Research roadmap

[The Research Roadmap Mandate](RESEARCH_ROADMAP_MANDATE.md) governs the 13 milestones, five phases, authorization and change control. The [task catalog](14_WORK_BREAKDOWN_STRUCTURE.csv) maps the 105 retained tasks to those milestones, and the [dependency table](17_TASK_DEPENDENCIES.csv) provides predecessor relationships. M01–M10 are accepted with conditions under the decisions summarized in the mandate and reaffirmed as the M11-R1 prerequisite. M11-R1 verification and readiness assessment are submitted for Project Director review. An Isaac Sim pilot, M12 and M13 require separate authorization.
